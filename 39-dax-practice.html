Module 3 · Practice

Fifteen problems. Attempt before you peek.

Five per tier, tiered to match the four Level pages before this one. Each problem is a <details> — write your own answer first, on paper or in Power BI, before opening it to check.

Tier A — after DAX Basics & Measures

Worked on AdventureWorksDW's FactInternetSales and DimProduct; take-away twins on the inbuilt CSVs.

Tier B — after Time Intelligence & Aggregations

AdventureWorksDW-only. The inbuilt CSVs each span one calendar year — not enough history for a genuine time-intelligence comparison, so these problems don't get CSV take-away twins; see the Time Intelligence page for the full reasoning.

Tier C — after CALCULATE, FILTER & Modeling

The capstone tier — CALCULATE, FILTER, and ALL doing real work, closing with a problem that asks you to re-derive a formula from two pages back.
