A report on your laptop helps nobody but you

The Power BI Service is where Desktop work becomes something a team can actually see and use — the "Service" half of the Desktop-vs-Service split from Module 1.

This page is a placeholder — full lesson content lands here as the class reaches this module. The framing below is real; the guided walkthrough, exercises, and quiz questions are still to come.

Publishing, in one click-path

Power BI Desktop→Home ribbon→Publish→choose a workspace→Publish→"Open in Power BI"

Publishing uploads the .pbix file's report and data model to a workspace in the Power BI Service (app.powerbi.com) — a shared cloud space a team works out of, distinct from any one person's personal "My Workspace."

Report vs. Dashboard, revisited

The Architecture page already drew this line: a Report is the multi-page thing built in Desktop; a Dashboard is a single-page pinboard that only exists in the Service, built by pinning individual visuals from one or more reports onto it.

Sharing a Report

Share → add people/groups by email, or a workspace's Members list for anyone who needs to build, not just view.

Sharing a Dashboard

Same Share mechanism, but a Dashboard can pull tiles from several different reports into one exec-friendly view.
