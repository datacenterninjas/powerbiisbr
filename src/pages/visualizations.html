05 · Visualizations

Eight charts. One rule: every chart answers a business question.

If you can't say what question a chart answers, don't build it. Click any card below to see steps, formatting, best practices, and common mistakes.

Which chart, when?

Business questionChartAvoid when…
Compare a number across categoriesBar ChartMore than ~10–12 categories
Show parts of a wholePie ChartMore than 5 categories (Rule of 5)
Show change over timeLine ChartComparing unrelated categories
Show one critical headline numberKPI CardYou need more than 4–6 things at once
Show progress toward a goalGauge ChartComparing multiple items at once
Show relationship between two numbersScatter PlotOnly one number available, <10 points
Show geographic distributionMapPrecise comparison matters more than location
Show hierarchy + proportion togetherTree MapOnly one category level exists

The eight charts, in depth

Formatting that makes it look professional

Theme

One click applies a consistent palette + font to the whole report. Pick it first, build after.

Real titles

Never ship "Sum of Sales by Category." Rename to what a CEO understands in 2 seconds.

Data labels

On for Bar/Pie/Card. Usually off for dense Line/Scatter — labels overlap into noise.

Conditional formatting

Red/green rules on tables and KPIs so problems jump out without reading every number.

Tooltips

Extra detail on hover without permanently cluttering the chart.

Interactions

Click one visual, watch the others filter — Power BI's biggest "wow" moment, on by default.
