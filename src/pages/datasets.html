Five small, purpose-built files feeding the Power Query, How Power Query Works, Joins & Data Modelling, M Query, M Language & End-to-End ETL, and KPIs & Dashboard pages — each one exists to teach a specific technique (merging, appending, unpivoting) rather than as a general practice dataset, so they're documented here on their own instead of in the Clean/Dirty table above.
