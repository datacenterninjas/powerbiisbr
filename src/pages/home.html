How to use this site

  • 1
    Follow along live. Keep this open on a second window while your instructor teaches — jump to the matching section from the sidebar.
  • 2
    Download the practice files. The Datasets section has every CSV used in class — both clean and deliberately messy versions — ready to open in Excel or load into Power BI.
  • 3
    Test yourself. The Self-Test Quiz has all 21 questions from class, fully interactive — click an answer and get instant feedback with the reasoning.
  • 4
    Come back later. Everything here — the chart-selection guide, the DAX formulas, the cheat sheet — is meant to outlast the workshop. Bookmark it.
