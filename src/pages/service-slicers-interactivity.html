What changes at scale

Everything about slicers taught on the Module 2 Slicers page still applies — this page is about what changes once a slicer's field has thousands of distinct values instead of five regions: a searchable list becomes mandatory, not a nicety; a numeric range slicer beats a giant checklist; and Sync Slicers across a multi-page published report is where "one control, every page" actually earns its keep for a real audience, not just a demo.

This module is also where publishing (earlier in Module 5) and interactivity (Module 2) meet — a synced slicer only feels seamless once the report actually lives in the Service where multiple people are clicking it at once.
