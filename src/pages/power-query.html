Real data is never clean. This is where you fix it.

Some analysts spend 70–80% of their time right here, not in charts. All 25 lessons below run on real practice files — mostly PQ_Practice_Orders.csv, plus its two companion tables for combining data and the original Sales_Dirty.xlsx for a few classics. Download them from the Datasets page and follow along.

25 lessons, real messy data

Every card follows the same shape: the before value, why it's a problem, the business impact of leaving it, the exact click-path to fix it, the after value, and what actually changed. Grouped the way you'll meet them in practice — cleaning text first, structure second, values and dates next, then rows, extraction, computed columns, and finally combining whole tables.

Never guess a fake number to hide a blank. Either fill it with a defensible business default, or remove the row — and always be ready to explain which choice you made and why.
