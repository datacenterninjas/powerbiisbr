Module 2 · Assignment

Make it interactive — five features, one AdventureWorksDW report

Same database as the rest of Module 1–2, one small report page — but now it has to respond when someone clicks on it. This is the assignment that separates "I can build a chart" from "I can build a report."

Worth 10 marks. Individual submission, through the LMS portal. Builds on AdventureWorksDW (the same database from the SQL Server & AdventureWorks page) — no DAX measures required anywhere in this assignment, that's Module 3. Every task below is achievable with drag-and-drop field wells and built-in aggregations (Sum/Average/Count) only.

The brief

Using your restored AdventureWorksDW database, build a single report page in Power BI Desktop that completes all five tasks below. Unlike the Visualizations page's worked examples, tasks in this assignment are free to pull from both DimProduct and FactInternetSales together — that's the whole point of a star schema, and exactly what Module 2's Interactivity and cross-filtering lessons are testing.

If a visual mixing the two tables shows blank or wrong numbers: open Model view (left-hand rail in Power BI Desktop) and confirm a relationship line connects DimProduct[ProductKey] to FactInternetSales[ProductKey]. Power BI usually autodetects it on load; if it didn't, drag one ProductKey column onto the other to create the relationship manually.

The five tasks

#TaskSkills from
1Build a report page with a Bar Chart (Sum of FactInternetSales[SalesAmount] by DimProduct[ProductLine]) and a KPI Card (Sum of FactInternetSales[SalesAmount]), plus a Slicer on DimProduct[Color]. Click a color in the slicer and confirm both other visuals cross-filter.Interactivity, Slicers
2Build a Matrix: Rows = DimProduct[ProductLine], Values = Count of ProductKey, Average of ListPrice, Average of StandardCost. Apply conditional formatting (a background color scale) to the Average List Price column.Tables & Matrix, Conditional Formatting
3Build a Bar Chart of DimProduct[EnglishProductName] by ListPrice, then apply a Top N filter (Filters pane → Top N → 5, ranked by List Price) so only the 5 most expensive products show.Filters
4Add one custom visual from AppSource (Get more visuals) and build it against any AdventureWorksDW field(s) of your choice — a Word Cloud of product names is an easy safe choice, but pick whichever one actually fits what you're showing.Custom Visuals
5Choose one: Group DimProduct[Color]'s individual colors into 3–4 broader buckets of your own naming (e.g. Neutral / Bright / Other) and chart the grouped field — or build a Histogram of DimProduct[ListPrice] with a bin size you choose and justify.Groups & Histograms

All five can live on one page, or be spread across a couple of pages — your call, as long as every task is present and working somewhere in the file.

How to submit

Two files, exact naming, both to the LMS portal:
  1. Your Power BI file, saved and submitted as YourFullName_Module2.pbix — e.g. Anjali_Kumar_Module2.pbix
  2. A single PDF containing a full-page screenshot of every visual, plus one screenshot showing the slicer mid-click (a color selected, other visuals visibly filtered), submitted as YourFullName_Module2_Screenshots.pdf

Capturing the "mid-click" screenshot: click a color in your slicer, then screenshot before clicking anywhere else — this is the one piece of evidence a static file can't show any other way, so don't skip it.
